# Open Questions

- How do exponentially weighted estimates compare after a covariance change?
- Does fitting the noise variance improve weak-factor detection without creating
  unstable threshold choices? Such tuning needs a separate development sample.
- Do robust covariance estimators improve the heavy-tail case?
- How sensitive are conclusions to asset dimension, factor count, and serial
  dependence beyond the current fixed grid?
- Do rolling cost and risk results persist across many independent paths?
- Can results be replicated on legally redistributable, point-in-time market
  data with explicit missing-data and universe rules?

These are untested follow-ups, not claims of completed work.
